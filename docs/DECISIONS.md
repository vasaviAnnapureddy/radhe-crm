# Decisions

A short log of choices and the reason for each. Newest at the bottom.

| # | Date | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01 Oct 2026 | React + Vite, not Next.js | The console sits behind a login, so search engines do not matter. Vite is simpler and faster to set up. |
| 2 | 01 Oct 2026 | Neon Postgres for both local and deployed | No Docker needed during development, which saves hours. Free plan is enough for about 1,600 units of data. |
| 3 | 01 Oct 2026 | Frontend always calls `/api` on its own address | Keeps the login cookie same-site. Avoids CORS and cross-site cookie problems after deploy. |
| 4 | 01 Oct 2026 | Read-only console | Fits the 4-day deadline. Only writes: login, logout, risk status. |
| 5 | 01 Oct 2026 | All calculations live in `backend/app/services/` | One tested place for every number. In V2 the same functions become the AI copilot's tools. |
| 6 | 01 Oct 2026 | Weights and thresholds in `config/*.yaml` | Scores and risk rules can be tuned without touching code. |
| 7 | 01 Oct 2026 | No AI features in this version | AI copilot is V2. Design is recorded in `docs/ROADMAP_V2.md`. |
| 8 | 01 Oct 2026 | `DOMAIN.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `ROADMAP_V2.md` are cut from `SPEC.md` by script | They cannot drift from the spec. If they ever differ, `SPEC.md` wins. |
| 9 | 01 Oct 2026 | Develop without Docker, ship with Docker (Phase 11) | Fast daily work; reviewers still get a one-command run. |
| 10 | 01 Oct 2026 | Backend loads all rows into memory (a "snapshot") and computes from there | The data is small (42,000 rows) and read-only. Hover cards become instant, and the same Python runs on the SQLite test database and on Postgres, so there are no SQL dialect surprises. For a much larger database, the functions in `services/` would be rewritten as SQL queries; their inputs and outputs stay the same. |
| 11 | 01 Oct 2026 | The backend describes tables, quick views and drawers (columns, facts, tabs, blocks) | The frontend needs one generic table, one hover card and one drawer for all 11 entity types, which keeps every page consistent and saves build time. |
| 12 | 01 Oct 2026 | Typed Pydantic models only for requests (login, risk status); replies are documented dictionaries | Typing about 40 reply shapes was not affordable in 4 days. The shapes are listed in `docs/PROGRESS.md` and checked by `tests/test_api.py`. |
| 13 | 01 Oct 2026 | Risks are detected at seed time and stored; only their status changes afterwards | Status changes must survive a restart. Reseed on demo morning so the day counts are fresh. |
| 15 | 01 Oct 2026 | UI components are hand-written on Radix primitives, not generated by the shadcn CLI | Same base and same approach as shadcn (you own the component code), but no interactive CLI step that could fail or change. The spec's HoverCard, Sheet, Command, Tabs are all there. |
| 16 | 01 Oct 2026 | `react-router` pinned to v7 and `@tanstack/react-table` to v8 | npm offered brand-new majors (8 and 9). The older majors are well known and stable; fewer surprises in a 4-day build. |
| 17 | 01 Oct 2026 | Fonts are bundled (`@fontsource-variable`) rather than loaded from Google Fonts | No request to an outside service; works offline and in Docker. |
| 18 | 02 Oct 2026 | Employee and customer portals added (changes "one admin, no portals" in the spec) | Developer request. One login page; the server decides the area by role. |
| 19 | 02 Oct 2026 | Portal safety is enforced on the server in two steps: `scope()` and `scrub()` | The browser can be made to ask for anything, so hiding links in the UI is not enough. Routes take no person id, each record is checked against the signed-in person's own list, and links to anything else are removed from the reply. |
| 20 | 02 Oct 2026 | Buyers get their own simple views, never the admin's customer view | The admin view holds health scores, risk notes and internal comments. |
| 21 | 02 Oct 2026 | All portal logins share one demo password, hashed once | Hashing 900 passwords one by one would make seeding slow. Fine for a demo; a real system needs a password per person and a reset flow. |
| 14 | 01 Oct 2026 | Login cookie is `SameSite=Lax`, httpOnly, and `Secure` in production | With `/api` on the same address as the frontend, this blocks cross-site requests without a separate CSRF token. |
